Our Glorious History

State Bank of India Staff Association (SBISA) is one of the historic trade union organisations representing employees of the State Bank of India.

The glorious journey of SBISA dates back to 1920, when the Imperial Bank of India Indian Staff Association was formed at Calcutta, before the enactment of the Trade Unions Act, 1926.

The early movement was shaped by pioneering leaders including Comrade Ajit Sen. The transformation of the Imperial Bank of India into the State Bank of India in 1955 marked an important turning point. On 22 February 1956, the Association adopted the name State Bank of India Staff Association (SBISA).
