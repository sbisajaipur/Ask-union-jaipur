Our Legacy

State Bank of India Staff Association carries a long tradition of collective representation, unity and service to employees of State Bank of India.

Our Journey

  • Roots of the organisation date back to 1920.
  • The association has continued its journey through major developments in Indian banking.
  • SBISA has represented the interests and aspirations of SBI employees.
  • Collective strength remains at the heart of the organisation.
