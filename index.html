About Union

Our Glorious History

State Bank of India Staff Association (SBISA) is one of the historic trade union organisations representing employees of the State Bank of India.

The glorious journey of SBISA dates back to 1920, when the Imperial Bank of India Indian Staff Association was formed at Calcutta, before the enactment of the Trade Unions Act, 1926. SBISA therefore carries a legacy of more than a century of collective struggle, solidarity and commitment to employee welfare.

The early movement was shaped by pioneering leaders including Comrade Ajit Sen, who played an important role in the development of the organisation.

The transformation of the Imperial Bank of India into the State Bank of India in 1955 marked an important turning point. On 22 February 1956, the Association adopted the name State Bank of India Staff Association (SBISA).

Our Legacy

Through generations, SBISA has worked for the legitimate interests, dignity and welfare of SBI employees.

  • Fair and equitable service conditions
  • Wage and settlement improvements
  • Medical and welfare benefits
  • Job security and career progression
  • Protection of employees' legitimate rights
  • Gender equality and safe workplaces
  • Effective employee representation
  • Dignity, justice and welfare

Our Organisation

SBISA has historically functioned through eight Circle organisations:

  • Kolkata
  • Bhubaneswar
  • Patna
  • North Eastern
  • Lucknow
  • Delhi
  • Chandigarh
  • Jaipur

Our Commitment

In an era of rapid technological and organisational change, SBISA continues to stand for the legitimate interests, dignity and welfare of SBI employees.
